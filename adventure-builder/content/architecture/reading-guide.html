Each view highlights a structure of the system and the concerns of its readers.

How to read a view

Views follow the architecture view template: Primary Presentation, Element Catalog, Context Diagram, Variability Guide, Rationale and Related Views. The diagram and catalog complement each other; related views help readers move from context to detail.

Reading paths by topic

  • Responsibilities and dependencies: Top level module uses, OPC decomposition, OPC uses and Workflow manager.
  • Workflows and integration: Top level SOA, Consumer website and OPC component & connector, followed by the two OPC interface contracts.
  • Data and infrastructure: Data model, Deployment and Installation.
  • Traceability: Mapping between views and Requirements mapping.

Reusing the templates

The architecture view template and interface template retain the original instructions. The download buttons on those pages provide Markdown outlines to fill in.

Source conventions

TODO marks incomplete content; TBD marks a decision still to be made; N/A marks a section that does not apply. These gaps were not filled during the reorganization.
